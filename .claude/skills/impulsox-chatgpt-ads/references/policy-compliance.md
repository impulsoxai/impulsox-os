# Policy & Compliance Guardrails

> Verified: 2026-05-30. Re-verify live policy docs before launch.
> Run before writing aggressive claims or regulated-category ads. Mandatory gate before delivery.

## Never do
- Guarantee income, profit, ranking, clicks, conversions, or outcomes.
- Imply OpenAI endorsement.
- Imply access to private ChatGPT user data.
- Target sensitive traits, vulnerabilities, or distress.
- Use fake scarcity, fake proof, fake testimonials, or fake benchmarks.
- Hide material terms on the landing page.
- Write claims the landing page cannot substantiate.
- Suggest evading review, crawlers, or policy checks.

## Claims review — ask for every claim
1. Is it true?
2. Can the landing page prove it?
3. Would a reasonable buyer understand the limits?
4. Could it be read as a guaranteed result?
5. Does it build trust or just heat?
If a claim fails any, rewrite it.

## Safer aggressive copy (positioning without false certainty)
- "The early operator playbook."
- "Build your first test before everyone copies the same angles."
- "Treat conversation intent like the new auction surface."

## Regulated categories — extra care
Health, finance/credit, employment, housing, and similar: require disclosures, avoid
outcome promises, and check the live policy docs before launch. When unsure, flag to
the operator rather than guessing.
